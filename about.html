Our Story

Grown with care, shared with love

Orgo started in 2014 when our founder, Amina, began selling surplus vegetables from her family farm at a weekend market. Neighbours kept coming back for the taste, and word spread quickly.

Today we work with more than 40 organic farms, delivering fresh produce across the city. Our promise has never changed: honest food, fair prices and respect for the land.

  • Certified organic since day one
  • Direct relationships with every farmer
  • Compostable, plastic-free packaging
